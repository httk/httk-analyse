httk.analyse.matsci¶

Materials-science analysis built on generic numerical primitives.

Every public name’s canonical, documentable home is its defining module (the result_type recorded by httk.analyse.summary.analysis_summary()); this package re-exports all public names of every matsci module as a convenience.

httk.analyse.matsci.charge_transition_levels(intercepts, fermi_interval, *, tolerance=1e-10)[source]¶

Return only charge crossings on the lower envelope in a finite interval.

Each intercept is the formation energy at EF=0; charge q gives the line intercept + q*EF. The intercepts MUST be formation energies at E_F = 0 measured from the valence-band maximum, that is defect_formation_energy(..., fermi_level=0.0).energy for each charge; the bound charge_transition_level defines its Fermi level relative to the VBM and nothing here can check that. Coincident crossings report every tied charge. Crossings at interval endpoints are excluded.

Parameters:

  • intercepts (collections.abc.Mapping[int, float]) – Nonempty mapping from distinct integer charges to eV intercepts.

  • fermi_interval (tuple[float, float]) – Increasing finite (low, high) bounds in eV.

  • tolerance (float) – Absolute eV tolerance for tie recognition.

Returns:

Stable transitions ordered by Fermi energy.

Raises:

ValueError – If charges, interval, intercepts, or tolerance are invalid.

Return type:

tuple[ChargeTransition, …]

httk.analyse.matsci.defect_formation_energy(defect_energy, host_energy, atom_deltas, chemical_potentials, *, charge, fermi_level, vbm, alignment, correction)[source]¶

Compute a charged defect energy from explicit eV references.

The expression is Edef-Ehost-sum(delta_n*mu)+q*(EF+VBM+alignment)+correction. Positive atom deltas add atoms; positive charge removes electrons.

alignment is the potential alignment ΔV = V_def(far from defect) - V_host(far) (Van de Walle-Neugebauer), added as written. Freysoldt/Kumagai-type correction values often already include the -q*ΔV alignment term; do not count it twice.

Parameters:

  • defect_energy (float) – Defect supercell total energy in eV.

  • host_energy (float) – Matching host supercell total energy in eV.

  • atom_deltas (collections.abc.Mapping[str, int]) – Exact signed integer atom changes by element.

  • chemical_potentials (collections.abc.Mapping[str, float]) – Required elemental reservoirs in eV per atom.

  • charge (int) – Exact integer charge state.

  • fermi_level (float) – Fermi energy in eV relative to the supplied reference.

  • vbm (float) – Valence-band reference energy in eV.

  • alignment (float) – Potential alignment ΔV = V_def(far) - V_host(far) in eV.

  • correction (float) – Explicit finite-size or other correction in eV, excluding alignment already counted.

Returns:

Immutable total and signed term decomposition.

Raises:

ValueError – If atom deltas, references, or energies are invalid.

Compute self or coherent scattering with exp(+i q·displacement).

Self scattering averages over atoms and origins. Coherent scattering is mean[conj(rho(q,t))*rho(q,t+lag)]/N, retaining the density peak at q=0. For arbitrary q use unwrapped positions; periodic wrapped coordinates are phase equivalent only for wavevectors commensurate with the fixed cell.

  • timestep (float) – Uniform frame spacing in ps.

  • wavevectors (Any) – Finite Cartesian (wavevectors, 3) array in inverse angstrom.

  • kind (Literal['self', 'coherent']) – Explicit self or coherent definition.

  • max_lag (int | None) – Largest inclusive lag.

Returns:

Complex correlations in lag and wavevector order.

Pressure is positive in compression and measured in GPa. This is an incremental hydrostatic criterion, not a general pre-stressed finite-strain stability test.

httk.analyse.matsci.bond_order(positions, cell, cutoff, degree, periodicity=(True, True, True))[source]¶

Compute Steinhardt q_l per atom and bond-weighted global Q_l.

Averages complex Y_lm over directed bonds, then takes sqrt(4*pi/(2*l+1) * sum_m abs(mean(Y_lm))**2). No neighbor-of-neighbor averaging or crystalline/liquid classification is implied.

The global Q_l pools directed bonds, so each pair contributes both r and -r. Since Y_lm(-r) = (-1)^l Y_lm(r), the global value is identically zero (to rounding) for odd l; it is meaningful for even l, as in Steinhardt’s definition. Per-atom q_l remains valid for odd l. The global value is bond-weighted (Steinhardt), not atom-averaged as in Lechner-Dellago or pyscal, so it differs from those codes for non-uniform coordination.

httk.analyse.matsci.equilibrium_response(*, temperature, ensemble, energies=None, enthalpies=None, volumes=None, block_size=None, remainder='raise')[source]¶

Estimate canonical or isothermal-isobaric fluctuation responses.

NVT uses total E and gives Cv=Var(E)/(kB*T²). NPT requires total H and V, giving Cp=Var(H)/(kB*T²), kappa=Var(V)/(kB*T*mean(V)), and volumetric alpha=Cov(V,H)/(kB*T²*mean(V)). Population moments use ddof=0. All samples receive equal weight and must represent equilibrium at the supplied T. Thermostat/barostat parameters or a linear drift check do not establish correct ensemble sampling. These formulas do not apply to NVE energy data.

E, H and V must be whole-system totals of the simulated cell, never per-atom or otherwise normalized values. Per-atom inputs (for example LAMMPS thermo_modify norm yes) divide Cv and Cp by N² and kappa and alpha by N; divide the whole-system results by N afterwards instead. NPT enthalpy is H = E_total + P_ext*V with E_total including kinetic energy and P_ext the fixed barostat set-point pressure. The LAMMPS enthalpy thermo keyword uses the instantaneous pressure instead and biases Cp and alpha.

Block standard errors assume independent blocks; choose block length much longer than the correlation time tau and check block-size sensitivity. Each block uses population moments about its own mean, so short blocks bias variances low by a factor (n_b - 1)/n_b plus about 2*tau/n_b, with tau the integrated correlation time in samples and n_b the block length in samples. The full estimate need not equal the mean block estimate.

Parameters:

  • temperature (float) – Positive fixed equilibrium temperature in K.

  • ensemble (Literal['NVT', 'NPT']) – Explicit NVT or NPT ensemble.

  • energies (collections.abc.Sequence[float] | None) – NVT whole-system total energies in eV; required for NVT only.

  • enthalpies (collections.abc.Sequence[float] | None) – NPT whole-system total enthalpies in eV; required for NPT only.

  • volumes (collections.abc.Sequence[float] | None) – NPT whole-system volumes in angstrom³, matching enthalpies.

  • block_size (int | None) – Optional samples per block, at least two; requires two complete blocks.

  • remainder (Literal['raise', 'drop']) – Raise on an incomplete block or explicitly drop the tail.

Returns:

Values named after property definitions: extensive heat_capacity_constant_volume or heat_capacity_constant_pressure in eV/K, isothermal_compressibility in GPa⁻¹ and volumetric_thermal_expansion in K⁻¹ (the NPT pair).
