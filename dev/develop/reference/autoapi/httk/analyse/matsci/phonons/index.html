httk.analyse.matsci.phonons.harmonic_thermodynamics(frequencies, temperatures, weights=None, *, zero_modes='raise', imaginary='raise', cutoff_frequency=0.0)[source]¶

Calculate harmonic free energy, energy, entropy and heat capacity.

Frequencies are signed THz values; weights are nonnegative mode counts and are never normalized automatically. Negative modes are imaginary modes.

Parameters:

  • frequencies (collections.abc.Sequence[float]) – One-dimensional frequencies in THz.

  • temperatures (collections.abc.Sequence[float]) – Nonnegative temperatures in K, retained in input order.

  • weights (collections.abc.Sequence[float] | None) – Optional nonnegative mode counts matching the frequencies.

  • zero_modes (Literal['raise', 'omit']) – Raise on zero frequencies or explicitly omit their weight.

  • imaginary (Literal['raise', 'omit']) – Raise on negative frequencies or explicitly omit their weight.

  • cutoff_frequency (float) – Nonnegative THz; modes with |frequency| below it count as zero modes, of either sign.

Returns:

Immutable properties in eV and eV/K, with mode exclusions reported.

Raises:

ValueError – If inputs are malformed, policies reject modes, or results are non-finite.

Return type:

HarmonicThermodynamics

httk.analyse.matsci.phonons.mode_gruneisen(volumes, frequencies, reference_volume=None)[source]¶

Fit log frequency against log volume for already matched positive modes.

Quadratic fits are used with three or more volumes, otherwise linear fits. There is no automatic branch or band matching.

Parameters:

  • volumes (collections.abc.Sequence[float]) – Distinct positive volumes in angstrom³.

  • frequencies (collections.abc.Sequence[collections.abc.Sequence[float]]) – Positive frequency matrix with shape (volume, mode), in THz.

  • reference_volume (float | None) – Positive derivative volume; defaults to the geometric mean.

Returns:

Per-mode -d log(omega)/d log(V) and design diagnostics.

Raises:

ValueError – If values are invalid, modes are nonpositive, or the fit is rank deficient.

Return type:

GruneisenFit

httk.analyse.matsci.phonons.quasiharmonic(volumes, static_energies, frequencies_by_volume, temperatures, weights=None, *, zero_modes='raise', imaginary='raise', cutoff_frequency=0.0)[source]¶

Fit static energy plus harmonic Helmholtz free energy at each temperature.

Frequency columns must identify the same modes at every volume. Temperatures must be strictly increasing so the endpoint-aware numerical derivative is well defined. The returned alpha_V uses numpy.gradient at endpoints.

Parameters:

  • volumes (collections.abc.Sequence[float]) – Distinct positive sampled volumes in angstrom³.

  • static_energies (collections.abc.Sequence[float]) – Matching static energies in eV on one extensive basis.

  • frequencies_by_volume (collections.abc.Sequence[collections.abc.Sequence[float]]) – Matched signed mode frequencies, one row per volume.

  • temperatures (collections.abc.Sequence[float]) – At least three strictly increasing nonnegative K values.

  • weights (collections.abc.Sequence[float] | None) – Optional common mode counts.

  • zero_modes (Literal['raise', 'omit']) – Explicit zero-mode policy.

  • imaginary (Literal['raise', 'omit']) – Explicit negative-frequency policy.

  • cutoff_frequency (float) – Nonnegative THz zero-mode cutoff, applied identically at every volume.

Returns:

Equilibrium volume, minimized free energy, bulk modulus and alpha_V.

Raises:

ValueError – If scans, mode exclusions or temperature data are inconsistent.

Return type:

QuasiHarmonicResult
