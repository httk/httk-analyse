class httk.analyse.matsci.eos_models.EOSFit[source]¶

An approximate static EOS fit with explicit relative weighting.

The weighted Jacobian condition measures numerical conditioning, not confidence in physical parameters. Inputs must use one static branch, composition and electronic/relaxation protocol. Residuals are observed minus predicted. Pressure is positive in compression.

Parameters:

  • model – EOS family.

  • equilibrium_volume – Minimum volume in angstrom³.

  • equilibrium_energy – Minimum energy in eV.

  • bulk_modulus – Equilibrium bulk modulus in GPa.

  • bulk_modulus_derivative – Dimensionless equilibrium pressure derivative.

  • volumes – Input volumes in caller order.

  • energies – Input energies in caller order.

  • weights – Positive relative least-squares weights in caller order.

  • residuals – Energy residuals in eV.

  • rmse – Unweighted energy root mean squared error in eV.

  • weighted_rmse – Weighted energy root mean squared error in eV.

  • condition_number – Condition number of the scaled weighted parameter Jacobian.

httk.analyse.matsci.eos_models.fit_eos(volumes, energies, *, model, weights=None)[source]¶

Fit BM3, Murnaghan or Vinet static energies with optional relative weights.

Requires httk-analyse[scipy]. Weights multiply squared residuals and need not sum to one. The initial NumPy BM3 fit must have an interior stable minimum; the nonlinear fit also requires an interior positive-modulus minimum and full Jacobian rank. Results contain no inferred error bars. Fit-window sensitivity and comparing physical protocols remain necessary.

Parameters:

  • volumes (collections.abc.Sequence[float]) – At least five distinct positive volumes in angstrom³.

  • energies (collections.abc.Sequence[float]) – Matching static energies in eV on the same extensive basis.

  • model (EOSModel) – Explicit EOS family to fit.

  • weights (collections.abc.Sequence[float] | None) – Positive finite relative weights, or equal weighting.

Returns:

Immutable fit with input-order residuals and numerical diagnostics.

Raises:

  • ImportError – If SciPy is unavailable.

  • ValueError – If data, weights, minimum, convergence or rank are invalid.

Return type:

EOSFit
