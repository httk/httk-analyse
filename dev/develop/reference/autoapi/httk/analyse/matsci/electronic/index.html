httk.analyse.matsci.electronic.integrate_dos(energies, density, lower, upper, *, spin_degeneracy)[source]¶

Integrate a piecewise-linear DOS between energy bounds.

DOS values are states/eV for one spin channel unless the caller supplies a different convention through spin_degeneracy. Bounds outside the grid are clipped to its endpoints and in-grid bounds are linearly interpolated.

Parameters:

  • energies (collections.abc.Sequence[float]) – Strictly increasing energy grid in eV.

  • density (collections.abc.Sequence[float]) – Nonnegative DOS values in states/eV on the declared spin basis.

  • lower (float) – Lower integration bound in eV.

  • upper (float) – Upper integration bound in eV.

  • spin_degeneracy (float) – Explicit multiplicative channel count, commonly 1 or 2.

Returns:

Integrated state count after applying spin_degeneracy.

Raises:

ValueError – If arrays, bounds, or spin degeneracy are invalid.

Return type:

float

httk.analyse.matsci.electronic.electron_count(energies, density, chemical_potential, *, temperature, spin_degeneracy)[source]¶

Integrate occupied DOS using Fermi-Dirac occupations.

At zero temperature the occupation is one below the chemical potential, zero above it, and one half exactly at the chemical potential. At finite temperature, the piecewise-linear DOS times occupation is integrated with thermally resolved Gaussian panels. The DOS spin basis and multiplier must be chosen explicitly by the caller.

Parameters:

  • energies (collections.abc.Sequence[float]) – Strictly increasing grid in eV.

  • density (collections.abc.Sequence[float]) – Nonnegative DOS in states/eV on the declared spin basis.

  • chemical_potential (float) – Chemical potential in eV.

  • temperature (float) – Temperature in K, including zero.

  • spin_degeneracy (float) – Explicit multiplier for spin channels represented.

Returns:

Integrated occupied electron count.

Raises:

ValueError – If inputs are invalid or the result is non-finite.

Return type:

float

httk.analyse.matsci.electronic.solve_chemical_potential(energies, density, target_electrons, *, temperature, spin_degeneracy, tolerance=1e-10, max_iterations=200)[source]¶

Find a chemical potential whose DOS integral matches a target count.

A bracket is expanded outside the grid for finite-temperature tails. At zero temperature, a count on a gap plateau returns a chemical potential within that plateau. The DOS integral is never renormalized to force the requested count.

Parameters:

  • energies (collections.abc.Sequence[float]) – Strictly increasing DOS grid in eV.

  • density (collections.abc.Sequence[float]) – Nonnegative DOS in states/eV on the declared spin basis.

  • target_electrons (float) – Desired electron count.

  • temperature (float) – Temperature in K, including zero.

  • spin_degeneracy (float) – Explicit multiplier for spin channels represented.

  • tolerance (float) – Absolute electron-count convergence tolerance.

  • max_iterations (int) – Maximum bisection iterations.

Returns:

Chemical potential in eV.

Raises:

ValueError – If target is unreachable, inputs are invalid, or convergence fails.

Return type:

float

httk.analyse.matsci.electronic.band_edges(energies, occupations, *, maximum_occupation, occupation_tolerance=1e-06, energy_reference)[source]¶

Classify sampled band edges using explicit occupations.

Arrays have shape (bands, k points). Energies are in eV and occupations use the same per-state convention as maximum_occupation. Band-path sampling is retained as supplied and is not a Brillouin-zone integration.

Occupations outside [0, maximum_occupation] by more than occupation_tolerance are rejected. Methfessel-Paxton smearing (VASP ISMEAR >= 1) can yield slightly negative or super-maximal occupations, so callers must clean them or choose occupation_tolerance accordingly. The analysis is per spin channel: the spin-polarised material gap is min(CBM over both spins) - max(VBM over both spins).

Parameters:

  • energies (collections.abc.Sequence[collections.abc.Sequence[float]]) – Finite band energies in eV.

  • occupations (collections.abc.Sequence[collections.abc.Sequence[float]]) – Finite occupations matching energies.

  • maximum_occupation (float) – Explicit maximum occupancy of a state.

  • occupation_tolerance (float) – Absolute tolerance at empty/full endpoints.

  • energy_reference (float) – Explicit energy to subtract from every band energy.

Returns:

Sampled valence/conduction extrema and sampled gaps.

Raises:

ValueError – If the arrays or occupation conventions are invalid.

Return type:

BandEdges

httk.analyse.matsci.electronic.fit_effective_mass(kpoints, energies, center)[source]¶

Fit a full local quadratic band and return its signed mass tensor.

Cartesian k coordinates use inverse angstroms with the 2π included (physical k, |k| = 2π/λ), energies use eV, and center uses the same Cartesian reciprocal-space basis. Reciprocal-lattice fractions or 1/λ coordinates give masses off by (2π)² ≈ 39.5. The local model is E=E0+g·dk+0.5*dk.T@H@dk. The conversion is m*/m_e = (hbar²/m_e)*H⁻¹, with hbar²/m_e in eV angstrom².

Parameters:

  • kpoints (collections.abc.Sequence[collections.abc.Sequence[float]]) – Cartesian sample vectors with shape (n, 3), in inverse angstroms including 2π.

  • energies (collections.abc.Sequence[float]) – Matching energies in eV.

  • center (collections.abc.Sequence[float]) – Expansion point in inverse angstroms, including 2π.

Returns:

Fitted Hessian, signed mass tensor, axes, and residual diagnostics.

Raises:

ValueError – If data are malformed, rank deficient, or the Hessian is singular.

Return type:

EffectiveMassFit
